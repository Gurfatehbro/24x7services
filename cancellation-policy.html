Cancellation Policy

Clear and Simple Service Booking Cancellation Terms

Thank you for choosing 24X7 Customer Care Support. Please read our cancellation policy carefully before booking or scheduling any home appliance repair service with us.

1. Service Cancellation

Customers may cancel a service booking request before technician dispatch without any cancellation charges. Simply call our toll-free helpline at 18002706013 to inform our desk.

2. Cancellation After Technician Dispatch

If a service request is cancelled after a certified technician has been assigned and dispatched to your location, standard visiting or inspection charges may apply to cover technician transit costs.

3. Same-Day Cancellation

Same-day cancellations after appointment confirmation may be subject to standard service processing charges if the technician has already reached your doorstep locality.

4. Cancellation by Company

While we strive to honor all scheduled visits promptly, we reserve the right to cancel or reschedule a service appointment due to:

  • Technician unavailability due to emergencies
  • Operational or weather constraints
  • Incorrect or unreachable customer contact details
  • Unforeseen circumstances beyond our control

In such cases, our team will proactively notify you and reschedule the visit at your earliest convenience.

5. Refund on Cancellation

If any advance payment has already been made and the cancellation qualifies for a refund under these terms, the full eligible amount will be processed back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days according to our Refund Policy.

6. Contact Us for Cancellations

To cancel or reschedule an existing service booking, please contact our 24x7 customer support helpline:

  • Helpline Toll-Free: 18002706013
  • Service Region: Delhi NCR, India
  • Website: https://www.helplinesupportdelhi.online
