Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to 24X7 Customer Care Support (accessible via https://www.helplinesupportdelhi.online). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect from customers requesting home appliance repair services, how that information is utilized, and the safeguards we take to ensure your data is handled securely.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a service booking enquiry or contact our customer support desk, we collect only the necessary details required to coordinate doorstep technician visits:

  • Full Name: To identify the customer and address service records.
  • Contact Number: To call you back, confirm appointments, and coordinate technician visits.
  • Service Address / Locality: To route our field technicians within Delhi NCR.
  • Appliance Details: Brand (e.g., LG, Samsung, Whirlpool), appliance category (Refrigerator, Washing Machine, Microwave, AC), and age of appliance to diagnose required repair tools and spare parts.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized for the following operational purposes:

  • Scheduling and fulfilling home appliance repair and maintenance services.
  • Contacting you regarding your service request, estimated visit time, and job status.
  • Generating transparent service estimates and receipts.
  • Customer service follow-up and quality assurance.

3. Data Protection and Confidentiality

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to third-party marketing agencies, lead aggregators, or unauthorized third parties. Your details are accessible solely by our direct support dispatch team and assigned field service technicians.

4. Cookies and Analytical Tracking

Our website utilizes standard, non-invasive cookies necessary for basic site navigation, form security, and performance monitoring. We do not use intrusive spyware or third-party tracking software.

5. Third-Party Links & Disclaimers

Our website may provide voluntary options to connect via phone or third-party communication channels (such as WhatsApp). Please review the privacy terms of any third-party communication applications independently. We are an independent third-party multi-brand service provider and not an official OEM brand entity.

6. Data Retention & User Rights

We retain your contact records only as long as required to complete active service requests and fulfill local accounting records. If you wish to review, update, or request deletion of your contact records from our system, please contact our helpline.
