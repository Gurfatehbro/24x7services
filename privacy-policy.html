Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026 | 24X7 Customer Support

Welcome to 24X7 Customer Care Support. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or book our doorstep home appliance services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or request a service:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address (if provided)
  • Home / Service Address in Delhi NCR
  • Appliance details and service request information (e.g. brand, appliance type, issue)
  • Information submitted through contact forms or enquiries
  • Website usage information such as IP address, browser type, and cookies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:

  • Provide appliance repair, maintenance, and support services
  • Schedule appointments and dispatch doorstep technicians
  • Contact customers regarding enquiries, visiting status, and updates
  • Improve website performance and customer experience
  • Maintain service and warranty records
  • Send service-related communications and receipts

3. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent customers' personal information to third-party marketing companies.

Information may be shared only:

  • With certified technicians or field service partners solely for providing requested repairs
  • When required by law, court order, or legal authorities
  • To protect our business rights, technician safety, and customer safety

4. Cookies Policy

Our website may use standard cookies to improve website functionality and user experience. Users can disable cookies through browser settings if preferred.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission method is 100% secure.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain optional links to third-party communication channels (such as WhatsApp). We are not responsible for their independent privacy practices or content.

7. Customer Rights

Users may request to:

  • Access their personal information on file
  • Update or correct their contact details
  • Request deletion of their personal information from our active records
