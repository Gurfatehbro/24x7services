About Us

Independent Multi-Brand Appliance Repair Services Across Delhi NCR

Reliable Doorstep Appliance Repair in Delhi NCR

24X7 Customer Care Support is an independent home appliance repair and maintenance service network. We connect homeowners and business establishments across Delhi NCR with experienced technicians specialized in post-warranty repair and diagnostics.

Modern household appliances are integral to daily life. When a refrigerator stops cooling, a washing machine refuses to spin, or an air conditioner breaks down during hot weather, prompt technical assistance is crucial. Our goal is to provide fast, doorstep diagnostics and repair with complete pricing transparency.

What We Do

🧊 Refrigerator Repair

Single-door, double-door, side-by-side, and inverter compressor troubleshooting, gas charging, and thermostat repairs.

🧺 Washing Machine Repair

Front load, top load, and semi-automatic drum repairs, motor replacement, PCB repair, and drain pump issues.

🍲 Microwave Oven Repair

Convection, grill, and solo microwave heating issues, magnetron replacement, panel keys, and turntable repairs.

❄️ Air Conditioner Service

Split and window AC deep jet cleaning, cooling coil repairs, PCB diagnostics, and gas leak identification.
