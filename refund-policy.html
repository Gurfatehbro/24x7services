Refund Policy

Transparent and Customer-Centric Service Refund Terms

Thank you for using 24X7 Customer Care Support. We aim to provide quality customer support and repair assistance services for home appliances in Delhi NCR. Please read our refund policy carefully.

1. Service Cancellation

  • Customers may cancel a service request before technician dispatch without any deduction.
  • Refund eligibility will depend on the stage of service processing.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued in the following situations:

  • Payment was deducted but the service request was not processed.
  • Duplicate payment was made for the same service request.
  • Service could not be provided due to technical or operational reasons from our side.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be applicable in the following cases:

  • Service has already been completed and verified by the customer.
  • Customer cancels after technician dispatch and doorstep inspection has been performed.
  • Incorrect or misleading information was provided by the customer regarding the appliance defect.
  • Issues outside the agreed scope of the booked repair service.

4. Processing Time

  • Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–7 business days.
  • The refund amount will be credited back through the original payment method (UPI, Bank Account, or Card).

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support desk with the following details:

  • Full Customer Name
  • Registered Contact Number
  • Service Booking Request Details / Invoice Number
  • Payment Transaction ID / Proof of Payment

6. Contact Information

For any queries or refund requests, reach out directly to:

  • Helpline Number: 18002706013
  • Service Region: Delhi NCR, India
  • Website: https://www.helplinesupportdelhi.online

7. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update or modify this Refund Policy at any time to remain aligned with operational standards and regulatory guidelines.
